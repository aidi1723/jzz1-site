Frequently Asked Questions

What aluminum systems does JZZ1 supply for Vietnam industrial parks vs residential villas?

For Vietnam industrial parks and commercial factories (Binh Duong, Bac Ninh, Hai Phong), JZZ1 supplies custom 6063-T5/6061-T6 extrusion profiles, industrial louvers, and stick/unitized curtain walls. For urban villas and luxury condos in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi, we supply European-style W70/W80 thermal break casement windows and CY156 acoustic sliding doors.

How do JZZ1 windows handle Hanoi humidity and Ho Chi Minh City monsoon rain?

Our W70, W80, and CY156 systems use stepped pressure-equalized drainage chambers, vulcanized EPDM triple weatherstrips, and 600 Pa water penetration testing to prevent rainwater seepage during heavy tropical storms.

Can Vietnam buyers claim preferential import duty using China-ASEAN Form E?

Yes. JZZ1 issues official China-ASEAN FTA Form E Certificates of Origin for shipments to Hai Phong, Cat Lai (Ho Chi Minh City), and Da Nang ports under HS 7610.10 and HS 7604.21, reducing import tariffs significantly.

What is the lead time for custom extrusion dies and assembled windows to Vietnam?

Custom extrusion die tooling takes 7–12 days, standard aluminum profile production takes 12–18 days, and assembled glazed window packages ship within 15–25 days from Qingdao Port.
