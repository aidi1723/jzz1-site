Regional Compliance & Engineering Standards

Architectural fenestration orders are engineered to regional environmental conditions and national building codes. Key performance criteria include verified wind load deflection (ULS/SLS), acoustic attenuation (STC/Rw ratings), solar heat gain coefficients (SHGC), and multi-barrier EPDM weather sealing for commercial and high-density residential developments.
