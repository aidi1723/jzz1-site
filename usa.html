Frequently Asked Questions: US Architectural Window & Door Sourcing

What window and facade systems does JZZ1 supply for United States projects?

JZZ1 supplies 6063-T6 thermal break aluminum casement, tilt-and-turn, awning, and fixed window walls, heavy-duty lift-and-slide patio doors, bi-folding doors, and unitized or stick curtain wall systems engineered to meet NFRC thermal parameters and AAMA/WDMA/CSA 101/I.S.2/A440 (NAFS) CW and AW structural classes.

How does JZZ1 address ASHRAE 90.1 and IECC energy codes across US Climate Zones 1–7?

For Southern cooling zones (Zones 1–3: Florida, Texas, California), we configure spectrally selective double Low-E glazing with SHGC ≤ 0.23–0.25 and optional SGP/PVB hurricane impact laminated glass. For Northern heating zones (Zones 4–7: New York, Chicago, Pacific Northwest), we utilize 24mm–34mm PA66 GF25 polyamide thermal breaks, warm-edge spacers, and Argon-filled double or triple IGUs achieving whole-window U-factors down to 0.24–0.28 BTU/(hr·ft²·°F).
