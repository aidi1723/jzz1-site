FAQ

  • Can railings, garden fences, and gates be coordinated as one package? Yes. Buyers can align the layout, dimensions, finish direction, gate openings, packing split, and project labels before production is confirmed.
  • What drawings or dimensions should buyers send? Send the site layout or marked drawing, total lengths, required heights, gate opening sizes, fixing conditions, quantity, and destination.
  • Can the finish and color be matched across the system? A coordinated finish and color direction can be discussed for railings, fence panels, posts, and gates, then confirmed against the approved specification or sample.
  • How are outdoor aluminum systems packed for project delivery? Packing, component grouping, labels, and shipment splits are confirmed around the project receiving and installation workflow.
