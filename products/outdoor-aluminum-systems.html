Bioclimatic Motorized Aluminum Louver Pergolas (6063-T5)

Precision-engineered 6063-T5 architectural louver pergolas featuring hidden internal gutter drainage, IP67 UL/CE-certified motors, optional LED lighting, wind/rain sensors, and motorized outdoor blinds rated up to 70 km/h wind resistance.

Engineering & Automation Specs

  • 6063-T5 Structural Profiles: Blades 175×45×1.6mm / 200×35×2mm (span up to 4500mm), beams up to 6000mm, posts 150×150×3mm, and 60mm/90mm gutters.
  • IP67 Motor Grade (UL & CE): Waterproof IP67 motorized louver actuation with remote control, optional wind/rain sensors, and integrated LED lighting.
  • Hidden Drainage System: Built-in gutter channels route rainwater internally through structural posts to prevent perimeter dripping.

Customization & Add-on Modules

  • LED Ambient Lighting: Perimeter dimmable warm-white (3000K) or RGB LED light strips integrated directly into frame profiles.
  • Motorized Outdoor Side Blinds: Semi-visible mesh & PVC side screens customizable up to 6000mm × 2800mm, rated up to 70 km/h wind resistance and UV protection factor 80+.
  • Sliding Glass Walls: Frameless 10mm tempered glass multi-slide panels for enclosed four-season luxury pavilions.

Intertek Verified Physical Testing

  • Flexural Properties (EN 15534-4 / EN 15534-1): Mean bending strength 28.5 MPa (min 27.8 MPa) after cyclic moisture exposure; mean deflection at 500 N of 1.07 mm (max 1.38 mm).
  • 28-Day Water Immersion (EN 15534-4 Section 4.5.5): Mean water absorption 4.40% (limit ≤ 7%) with 0.11% length swelling.
  • Pendulum Slip Resistance (CEN/TS 15676): Horizontal direction pendulum test mean 81 (min 80) for wet outdoor walkways.

Durability & Environmental Metrics

  • Reaction to Fire (EN 13501-1:2018 / EN ISO 9239-1): Tested and classified as Class Bfl flooring fire performance in Intertek Report 220803005SHF-001.
  • Dimensional & Structural Stability: Tested across 300mm test spans under cyclic moisture resistance with only -3% bending strength variation.
  • FSC & EPD Certified: Verified under The International EPD System (S-P-10194, valid through 2028-07-31) and FSC Chain of Custody (GFA-COC-007174).

FAQ

  • Can railings, garden fences, and gates be coordinated as one package? Yes. Buyers can align the layout, dimensions, finish direction, gate openings, packing split, and project labels before production is confirmed.
  • What drawings or dimensions should buyers send? Send the site layout or marked drawing, total lengths, required heights, gate opening sizes, fixing conditions, quantity, and destination.
  • Can the finish and color be matched across the system? A coordinated finish and color direction can be discussed for railings, fence panels, posts, and gates, then confirmed against the approved specification or sample.
  • How are outdoor aluminum systems packed for project delivery? Packing, component grouping, labels, and shipment splits are confirmed around the project receiving and installation workflow.
