Why Contractors Prefer Casement Over Sliding in High-Spec Builds

While sliding windows offer space-saving simplicity, 6063-T5 casement windows deliver superior physical performance required for commercial energy and acoustic certifications:

  • Compression Gasket vs Weatherstrip Pile: Sliding windows utilize woven pile weatherstripping that permits higher micro-drafts under negative pressure. Casement sashes leverage active lever compression against automotive-grade vulcanized EPDM gaskets, stopping drafts and wind howl completely.
  • Structural Wind Deflection: Reinforced casement mullions with internal stiffeners handle cyclic wind gusts up to 4000 Pa without frame racking or air seal detachment.
  • Integrated Diamond / 304 SS Security Mesh: Optional inward or outward integrated stainless security flyscreens protect openings from insects and forced entry without obstructing daylight.

Frequently Asked Questions: 6063-T5 Thermal Break Casement Windows

Why specify 6063-T5 alloy for thermal break casement windows?

6063-T5 is the international architectural standard for fenestration extrusions. It provides optimal tensile strength (minimum 160 MPa), yield strength (minimum 110 MPa), superior dimensional tolerance for mitered corner joints, and excellent surface receptive quality for Qualicoat Class 2 powder coating and 25-micron architectural anodizing.

What thermal transmittance (U-factor / Uw) do your 6063-T5 casement windows achieve?

With 24mm to 34mm PA66 GF25 multi-chamber polyamide thermal breaks and double Low-E Argon-filled IGUs, JZZ1 casement windows achieve whole-window Uw of 1.2 to 1.5 W/(m²·K) (U-factor 0.21–0.26 BTU/(hr·ft²·°F)). With triple-glazed passive configurations, Uw reaches 0.85 to 1.05 W/(m²·K).

What is the MOQ, production lead time, and container loading capacity?

Our factory MOQ starts from 10 sets (sample orders supported). Standard production lead time is 7 to 25 days depending on profile finish and glass build-up. A standard 20GP container accommodates approximately 120–150 assembled sets; a 40HQ container accommodates 280–350 sets depending on opening dimensions.
