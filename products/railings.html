FAQ

  • Do you provide railing systems for balconies and stairs? Yes. JZZ1 supplies balcony, terrace, stair, deck, and related railing systems for residential and commercial applications.
  • What project requirements should be shared before a railing quotation? Share the required height, layout, fixing condition, finish direction, quantity, destination, and any buyer-side standard or performance requirement.
  • What technical inputs should buyers send before quoting railings? Buyers should send a layout or dimension drawing, overall length, railing height, mounting method, finish, quantity, and destination details.
  • Why do mounting method and layout matter in railing sourcing? They affect component planning, fixing logic, panel splits, and packing, so they should be defined before final quotation.
  • Do you support project packing and labels? Yes. Project packing, component grouping, and labels can be discussed around the receiving and installation workflow.
