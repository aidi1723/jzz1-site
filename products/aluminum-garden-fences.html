Posts, spacing, corners, and ground conditions

A fence quotation should start with a marked layout rather than a single panel size. The drawing should identify total runs, corners, changes in ground level, gate openings, and the surface receiving each post. Final panel splits and fixing details are reviewed against that input.

Coordinate a matching garden gate

Where the fence includes a pedestrian or access opening, share the clear opening width, required height, swing direction, and nearby post positions. The gate route can then align the visible bar spacing and finish direction with the fence.

FAQ

  • What information is needed to quote an aluminum garden fence? Send the fence layout, total length, required height, panel style, post spacing direction, ground or fixing condition, finish, quantity, and destination.
  • Can garden fences follow corners, slopes, or changing ground conditions? Corners, level changes, and ground conditions should be marked on the layout so panel splits, post positions, and fixing details can be reviewed before quotation.
  • Can the fence be supplied with a matching aluminum garden gate? Yes. The gate opening, bar spacing, visible style, and finish direction can be coordinated with the fence after drawings and dimensions are confirmed.
  • How are fence panels, posts, and project labels organized for shipment? The packing split, panel and post grouping, protection, and project labels are confirmed around the receiving and installation sequence.
