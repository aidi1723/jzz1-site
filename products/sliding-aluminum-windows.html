Frequently Asked Questions: 6063-T5 Thermal Break Sliding Windows

What makes 6063-T5 thermal break sliding windows water-resistant during storms?

JZZ1 sliding systems utilize a high-low stepped threshold profile (50mm to 65mm water dam) combined with concealed directional drain weep holes and baffle check valves. This prevents wind-driven rain backflow under cyclic pressures up to 600 Pa (AS 2047 / ASTM E547 test standard).

Can sliding aluminum windows incorporate integrated security screens?

Yes. Our 3-track sliding window systems feature a dedicated exterior track fitted with high-tensile 304 or 316 marine-grade stainless steel diamond mesh (0.8mm wire gauge), providing forced-entry security and mosquito protection without interfering with the glass sashes.

What is the MOQ and production turnaround for container orders?

MOQ starts from 10 sets for residential packages. Standard production lead time is 7 to 25 days. A 40HQ container accommodates approximately 300 to 380 knocked-down or semi-assembled sliding window sets, optimized for ocean shipping.

What hardware and roller ratings are used on your sliding window sashes?

We specify dual-bearing 304 stainless steel tandem rollers with nylon outer tires, rated for 80 kg to 150 kg per sash. The heavy-duty rollers ensure smooth, whisper-quiet operation across 100,000+ opening cycles.
