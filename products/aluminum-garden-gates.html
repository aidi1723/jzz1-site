Opening and hardware inputs

  • Clear opening width and height
  • Viewed side, hinge side, and swing direction
  • Ground clearance and nearby obstacles
  • Latch, lock, handle, and stop requirements
  • Adjacent post, wall, or fence condition

Frame, infill, hinges, locks, and handles

The gate frame and visible infill should be confirmed together with the hinge side and lock direction. Buyers should identify any required hardware brand or access condition before production. Automatic, sliding, or motorized operation is not included on this page.

Match the fence system

For a coordinated boundary, provide the fence panel drawing and gate opening in the same layout. This allows the visible bar spacing, top-line direction, posts, decorative elements, and finish reference to be reviewed as one system.

FAQ

  • What dimensions are needed to quote an aluminum garden gate? Send the clear opening width and height, nearby post or wall condition, required ground clearance, leaf direction, quantity, and destination.
  • Can a garden gate match the fence panel style and color? Yes. The frame, vertical-bar spacing, decorative direction, and finish reference can be coordinated with the adjacent fence after the layout is confirmed.
  • What hardware details should be confirmed before production? Confirm the hinge direction, opening frequency, latch or lock requirement, handle direction, ground stop if required, and any buyer-specified hardware.
  • How are gate leaf direction and opening clearance confirmed? Mark the viewed side, hinge side, swing direction, approach area, and nearby obstacles on the drawing so the opening logic can be checked before production.
