Certified Metal Door Manufacturing, Safety Glazing & Coating Traceability

Specifying exterior entry doors requires verified mechanical endurance, forced-entry resistance, and weather-tightness. Every JZZ1 entrance door order is backed by official third-party credentials:

  • Dual ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management Systems (10425Q00700R0S & 39726Q00006R001): Audited under CNAS and IAF MLA specifically covering the production of Metal Doors, Metal Guardrails, and architectural fenestration systems.
  • AS 2047-2014 (SMK41678), AS/NZS 2208 (CSi 7632) & US NFRC (CPD: ESD-M-1): Vision lites, sidelights, and transom windows utilize certified Grade A toughened or laminated safety Low-E IGUs.
  • AkzoNobel IATF 16949 (0561802) & PPG Cationic E-Coat (CP525D): Door frames and flush aluminum panels are protected byPPG electrophoretic epoxy primer and AkzoNobel Interpon architectural powder coatings (LRQA 10559523) or sublimated wood-grain finishes.

Hardware, Security & Threshold Engineering

  • Multi-Point Hook & Bolted Security: Standard single-latch locks allow tall aluminum door leaves to bow under thermal expansion or wind pressure. JZZ1 installs full-height stainless steel 5-point espagnolette locks with opposing security hooks that pull the leaf tightly into the EPDM compression gasket.
  • Automatic Drop-Down Acoustic & Draft Seal: Concealed bottom plunger seals automatically drop 8–12mm onto the threshold when the door closes, blocking wind-driven rain, dust, insects, and corridor noise (achieving STC 34–39 dB).
  • ADA / Wheelchair-Accessible Low Thresholds: Choose between stepped storm-dam sills (450–600 Pa water rating) or 15mm low-profile thermally broken barrier-free thresholds for commercial and hospitality compliance.

Custom Configurations: Sidelights, Transoms & Smart Locks

  • Compound Entry Assemblies: Single leaf, unequal double leaf (mother-and-son door), symmetric double swing, with fixed laminated glass sidelights and overhead transom windows engineered in a single structural sub-frame.
  • Smart Lock Pre-Milling: CNC-machined lock mortises and concealed cable transfer loops pre-prepped at the factory for Yale, Schlage, Samsung, or custom Wi-Fi/biometric smart locks and magnetic strikes.
  • Export Packaging: Each entrance door is factory hang-tested, wrapped in protective film and EPE foam, fitted with pull-bar protection boxes, and crated in ISPM 15 fumigated plywood cases.

Frequently Asked Questions: Aluminum Entrance Doors

What certifications cover JZZ1 aluminum entrance and pivot doors?

JZZ1 entrance doors are manufactured under dual ISO 9001:2015 certifications—specifically Certificate 10425Q00700R0S covering Metal Doors and Metal Guardrails and Certificate 39726Q00006R001 covering building materials—alongside Australian Standard AS 2047 (SMK41678), AS/NZS 2208 Safety Glazing (CSi 7632), and AkzoNobel IATF 16949 (0561802) surface coating traceability.

What is the maximum door leaf size and weight for luxury pivot entrance doors?

Using heavy-duty concealed hydraulic pivot boxes and 2.5mm–3.0mm 6063-T6 reinforced stiles, JZZ1 pivot entrance doors support single leaf widths up to 1800mm, heights up to 3200mm, and leaf weights up to 250–350 kg.

Can entrance doors integrate smart locks, multi-point hooks, and thermal break cores?

Yes. Our ED75 and ED85 thermal break entrance door series feature 24mm–34mm PA66 GF25 polyamide thermal barriers, 5-point stainless steel hook-and-deadbolt security locks, and optional biometric fingerprint/keypad smart lock integration.

What is the MOQ and lead time for custom project entrance doors?

MOQ starts from 10 sets (or mixed with a full villa window and door package). Typical production lead time is 12 to 25 days after CAD shop drawing approval.
