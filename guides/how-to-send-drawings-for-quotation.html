How to Send Drawings for Aluminum Window Quotation

A clear opening schedule cuts quotation turnaround from days to hours. Send AutoCAD DWG, PDF elevations, or Excel schedules to receive an itemized factory BOM, shop drawing takeoff, and FOB/CIF container estimate within 12–24 hours.

Direct Answer: The 5-Input Fast-Quote Formula

To get an accurate factory-direct quotation in 12–24 hours, every RFQ should include: (1) Width × Height dimensions, (2) Quantity per window/door tag, (3) Opening style (casement, tilt-turn, sliding, bi-fold), (4) Destination port & code (e.g., US NFRC, AU AS 2047, SG BCA), and (5) Glass & finish preference (e.g., 5+12A+5 Low-E, AkzoNobel matte black). Common Drawing Mistakes That Delay Quotes

  • Missing exterior vs interior view convention: Always note whether opening arrows are drawn looking from the outside or inside.
  • Unspecified glass safety rules: Floor-to-ceiling doors and wet-area windows typically require tempered or laminated safety glass (e.g., AS/NZS 2208 or SGCC).
  • Ignoring container height/width limits: Oversized curtain wall panels or 3m+ tall sliding doors should be flagged early for 40HQ vs Open-Top container planning.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum drawing information needed for quotation?

At minimum, buyers should send opening dimensions (width × height in mm or inches), quantity by model, opening style, destination port, and preferred glass/finish direction. JZZ1 returns itemized CAD takeoffs within 12 to 24 hours.

Can buyers send PDF elevations or screenshots instead of AutoCAD DWG files?

Yes. PDF architectural elevations, Excel opening schedules, and clearly labeled screenshots with dimensions are all sufficient to generate an initial BOM quotation and preliminary Proforma Invoice.

How can I estimate my project budget and container CBM before sending drawings?

Use JZZ1's free Interactive Proforma Invoice (PI) & CBM Estimator at jzz1.com/import-customs-clearance-guide.html to enter rough window dimensions, toggle FOB/CIF/DDP Incoterms, and check 20GP or 40HQ container fit instantly.

Why do incomplete drawings slow down factory quotation?

Missing opening directions (inward vs outward), unknown glass thickness (double Low-E vs laminated impact), or unspecified destination wind-load codes (NFRC/AS 2047) force multiple clarification rounds before accurate pricing can be locked.
