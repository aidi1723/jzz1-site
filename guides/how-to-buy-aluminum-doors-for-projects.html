How to Buy Aluminum Doors for Projects

Sourcing architectural aluminum doors for apartments, villas, hotels, and commercial buildings requires matching the right door system—heavy sliding, bi-fold, or pivot entrance—to your wind load, acoustic, and threshold requirements before production starts.

Direct Engineering Answer

To buy project aluminum doors without delays, confirm system type, exact opening schedule (mm), glass safety spec, hardware grade, and crate labeling upfront. For high-rise balconies and typhoon/acoustic zones, JZZ1 specifies the JZZ1-CY156 Side-Compression Heavy Sliding Door (> 4500 Pa wind load, STC 38–44 dB); for wide-span villa openings, use bottom-rolling bi-fold doors; and for main entrances, use thermal break pivot or ME90/D75/D95 swing doors backed by ISO 9001:2015 certification (10425Q00700R0S).

What to Confirm Before Ordering

  • Door Schedule & Handing: Left/right swing, inward vs outward opening, and clear egress width.
  • Safety Glazing Code: Toughened or laminated safety glass (e.g., AS/NZS 2208 CSi 7632 or ASTM/SGCC) for full-height door panels.
  • Surface Finish Durability: AkzoNobel Interpon D2000 (Qualicoat Class 2) or D3000 PVDF + PPG CP525D E-Coat for coastal salt-spray resistance.
  • Project Crate Labels: Specify unit/floor tags (e.g., VILLA-04 / GF-PATIO) for streamlined jobsite unloading.

Frequently Asked Questions

What information is needed to quote project aluminum doors?

Door type (sliding, bi-fold, pivot, or swing), exact opening dimensions, quantity, glazing build-up (double Low-E or SGP laminated), hardware brand preference, finish, destination port, and crate labeling rules.

When should contractors choose CY156 side-compression sliding doors over bi-fold doors?

Choose CY156 side-compression sliding doors (156mm frame depth, >4500 Pa wind load, STC 38–44 dB) for high-rise balconies, coastal resorts, and typhoon zones requiring maximum acoustic and weather sealing. Choose bottom-rolling bi-fold doors for ground-floor villas and restaurants needing 90%+ unobstructed opening width.

Can project buyers mix door sizes and systems in one container order?

Yes. Mixed door schedules and combined window-plus-door FCL shipments are standard at JZZ1, with room/floor barcode labels on every crate to simplify jobsite unloading.

Use JZZ1's Interactive Proforma Invoice (PI) & CBM Calculator to input door widths, heights, and quantities, compare FOB Qingdao vs CIF/DDP pricing, and check 20GP or 40HQ container utilization.
