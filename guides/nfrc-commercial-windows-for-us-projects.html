Executive Engineering Summary

Key Takeaway for US General Contractors, Specifiers & Importers

Commercial fenestration for US multi-family, hospitality, office, and mixed-use towers requires simultaneous compliance with NFRC thermal standards (National Fenestration Rating Council) and AAMA / WDMA / CSA 101/I.S.2/A440 (NAFS) structural performance classes. JZZ1 engineers heavy-duty 6063-T6 thermal break aluminum window systems utilizing 24mm to 34mm polyamide (PA66 GF25) thermal struts, warm-edge stainless/composite spacers, and double or triple Low-E insulated glass units (IGUs) filled with 90% Argon. Systems achieve whole-window U-factors from 0.24 to 0.32 BTU/(hr·ft²·°F) (1.36 to 1.81 W/m²K) and Design Pressure ratings from DP 50 to DP 80+ psf, meeting strict ASHRAE 90.1-2022 and IECC 2024 energy codes across all US Climate Zones 1 through 7.

ASHRAE 90.1 Climate Zones 1–3 (Southern US)

Target Markets: Florida, Texas, Southern California, Arizona, Georgia, Carolinas.

  • Primary Objective: Solar heat rejection and high-velocity wind/storm resistance.
  • Required SHGC: ≤ 0.23 – 0.25 to dramatically slash peak HVAC chilling loads.
  • Glazing Package: 6mm Solar-Control Low-E (surface #2) + 12mm Argon cavity + 6mm Clear tempered glass.
  • Impact / Hurricane Resilience: Optional SGP / PVB laminated inner lite engineered to ASTM E1886/E1996 large missile impact criteria for coastal zones.

ASHRAE 90.1 Climate Zones 4–7 (Northern US & Cold Highlands)

Target Markets: New York, New Jersey, Illinois, Massachusetts, Washington, Colorado.

  • Primary Objective: Maximum thermal insulation, sub-zero draft elimination, and zero frame condensation.
  • Required U-Factor: ≤ 0.26 – 0.28 BTU/(hr·ft²·°F) (≤ 1.48–1.59 W/m²K).
  • Glazing Package: Triple insulated glass units (e.g. 5Low-E + 12Ar + 5Clear + 12Ar + 5Low-E) with warm-edge composite spacers (Technoform).
  • Thermal Frame: 75mm architectural system with polyamide thermal barrier and foam-filled cavities.

Frequently Asked Questions: NFRC Commercial Windows for US Projects

What are the required NFRC metrics for commercial aluminum windows in the US?

The primary NFRC metrics are U-Factor (NFRC 100, measuring whole-window thermal heat loss, typically 0.26 to 0.35 BTU/(hr·ft²·°F)), Solar Heat Gain Coefficient or SHGC (NFRC 200, typically 0.22 to 0.40 depending on ASHRAE climate zones), Visible Transmittance or VT (0.45 to 0.65), Air Leakage (NFRC 400 / ASTM E283 ≤ 0.30 cfm/ft²), and Condensation Resistance or CRF (NFRC 500 ≥ 55 to 70).

How does JZZ1 meet NAFS CW and AW commercial performance classes?

JZZ1 fabricates commercial windows using heavy-wall 6063-T6 architectural aluminum alloy extrusions (1.8mm to 2.5mm+ wall thickness) with polyamide PA66 GF25 thermal breaks. Systems are engineered and tested to AAMA/WDMA/CSA 101/I.S.2/A440 for Commercial Window (CW) and Architectural Window (AW) classes, achieving Design Pressures from DP 50 psf (2400 Pa) up to DP 80+ psf (3800 Pa) for mid-rise and high-rise commercial envelopes.

How should US commercial buyers specify windows across ASHRAE Climate Zones 1 through 7?

For Southern warm zones (Zones 1–3: Florida, Texas, Southern California), solar heat mitigation is critical, requiring low SHGC (≤ 0.23–0.25) with double Low-E and heavy wind/hurricane impact resistance. For Northern cold zones (Zones 4–7: New York, Chicago, Upper Midwest, Pacific Northwest), maximizing insulation is paramount, requiring U-factors ≤ 0.26–0.28 BTU/(hr·ft²·°F) achieved via 24mm+ warm-edge Argon-filled IGUs and multi-chamber thermal breaks.
