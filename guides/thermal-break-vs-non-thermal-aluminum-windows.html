Executive Engineering Summary

Key Takeaway for B2B Importers, Contractors & Developers

Non-thermal windows utilize solid architectural aluminum extrusions (6063-T5 / T6) without an internal polyamide thermal barrier. For tropical, coastal, and mild regions (Southeast Asia, Caribbean, Middle East, and Mediterranean summer climates), non-thermal break aluminum windows provide identical structural strength, wind pressure resistance (tested to AS 2047 up to 4000 Pa ULS), and water tightness as thermal break systems, while delivering a 30% to 45% cost advantage on profile extrusion packages. Thermal break windows, incorporating PA66 GF25 reinforced insulation strips, are mandatory only where sub-zero winters, stringent heating energy codes, or strict condensation mitigation are required.

When Non-Thermal Windows Are the Superior Choice

  • Tropical & Warm Climates: Where heating is never required and ambient seasonal temperature fluctuations remain between 22°C and 35°C.
  • Budget-Optimized Commercial & Residential Projects: Allows contractors and developers to maximize glazing specs (e.g. adding Low-E coating or acoustic PVB) using the savings from non-thermal profile extrusions.
  • High Wind & Typhoon Resistance: Solid non-thermal profiles offer uncompromised torsional rigidity for high-rise coastal villas and apartment corridors.
  • Interiors, Partitions & Shaded Balconies: Enclosed sunrooms, balcony enclosures, and commercial office shopfronts.

When Thermal Break Windows Are Essential

  • Cold Winters & Temperate Climates: Sub-zero external temperatures create massive heat loss through non-insulated aluminum frames.
  • Condensation Prevention: High indoor humidity combined with cold outdoor glass causes water runoff and mold unless thermal barriers are present.
  • Strict Green Building Codes: Projects requiring LEED, Passive House, BCA Green Mark Platinum, or strict municipal U-value caps ($U_w \le 2.0 \text{ W/m}^2\text{K}$).
  • Luxury High-End Residences: Where maximum thermal comfort and zero radiative chill from frames are mandated by architectural specs.

Multi-Point Lock Security on Non-Thermal Windows

A common misconception among buyers is that non-thermal windows only use basic crescent latches or single-point catches. At JZZ1, all non-thermal aluminum sliding and casement windows are engineered with precision standard European euro-groove profile cavities that accept heavy-duty multi-point locking systems:

Casement Window Multi-Point Transmission

Gearbox-driven transmission rods engage mushroom cam pins into steel keepers along the top, side, and bottom of the frame, providing airtight EPDM gasket compression and certified anti-burglary protection.

Sliding Window Flush Hook Multi-Point Locks

Heavy-duty multi-point hook locks engage at multiple vertical points on the interlock stile, preventing sashes from being pried open from exterior balcony decks while maintaining slim visual sightlines.

Frequently Asked Questions: Non-Thermal & Thermal Break Windows

When are non-thermal windows the best engineering choice?

Non-thermal windows are the ideal choice for tropical and sub-tropical regions (such as Southeast Asia, the Caribbean, and coastal areas) where winter freezing does not occur. They offer identical structural strength, weather resistance, and wind pressure performance (tested up to 4000 Pa) at 30% to 45% lower extrusion cost compared with thermal break systems.

Can non-thermal windows be fitted with multi-point security locks?

Yes. Non-thermal aluminum casement and sliding windows can be equipped with European standard groove multi-point locks (CMECH, Hoppe, Siegenia, Kinlong) for enhanced security, perimeter air compression, and acoustic insulation.

When are thermal break windows necessary?

Thermal break windows are necessary for colder climates, strict municipal energy code compliance, high indoor-outdoor temperature differentials, and projects requiring rigorous condensation control.

What alloy and finishes are used for non-thermal export windows?

JZZ1 fabricates non-thermal windows using 6063-T5 or 6063-T6 architectural grade aluminum extrusions with wall thicknesses of 1.4mm to 2.0mm+, finished with Qualicoat-certified architectural powder coating, fluorocarbon PVDF, or anodized finishes.
