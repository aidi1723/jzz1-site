Executive Engineering Summary

Acoustic Isolation Engineering for High-Rise Corridors & Urban Facades

Standard sliding doors typically leak sound through perimeter gaps and single-pane resonance, resulting in poor noise insulation (STC ≤ 28 dB). An engineered acoustic sliding door solves urban traffic noise, MRT vibration, and aircraft sound through a triad of specialized components: asymmetric acoustic laminated glass with acoustic PVB interlayers, continuous co-extruded EPDM multi-lip compression gaskets, and multi-point compression locks that pull the sash hermetically into the frame during closing. JZZ1 manufactures acoustic sliding doors reaching certified test performance of STC 35 dB to STC 44 dB (Rw 36 to 45 dB), compliant with Singapore BCA Green Mark and international hospitality standards.

Engineering Features of JZZ1 Acoustic Sliding Doors

  • Acoustic PVB Interlayer: Viscoelastic acoustic PVB film absorbs sound vibrations and breaks coincidence frequency dips that standard glass cannot mitigate.
  • Multi-Chamber EPDM Gaskets: Automotive-grade EPDM gaskets around all sash perimeters replace porous nylon brush strips at critical air-leak points.
  • Heavy-Duty Multi-Point Compression Locks: Multi-hook locks draw the sliding panel 4mm to 6mm tighter against the jamb gasket upon latching.
  • Lift-and-Slide (Hebe-Schiebe) Option: Lowering mechanism drops the entire door weight onto high-durometer EPDM bottom seals when closed.
  • Heavy Stainless Steel Bogie Rollers: Dual tandem stainless steel wheels support acoustic glass sashes up to 400 kg with effortless, whisper-quiet gliding.

How to Prepare an RFQ for Acoustic Sliding Doors

Frequently Asked Questions: Acoustic Sliding Doors

What makes a sliding door acoustic and soundproof?

An acoustic sliding door achieves sound isolation (STC 35 to 44+ dB) through three engineered elements: 1) Heavy-duty acoustic laminated glass with sound-dampening PVB or SentryGlas interlayers; 2) Multi-chamber EPDM compression gaskets and double-fin brush seals that eliminate perimeter sound flanking; 3) Multi-point lift-and-slide or hook lock hardware that pulls the door sash tightly against the perimeter frame.

What STC rating is required for Singapore condos near expressways or MRT tracks?

Condo developments and hotels adjacent to busy Singapore expressways (PIE, CTE, AYE) or elevated MRT tracks typically specify an acoustic rating of STC 38 dB to 42 dB (Rw+Ctr 32 to 36 dB). This is typically achieved with double glazed units incorporating an acoustic laminated outer pane (e.g., 8.76mm acoustic lam + 12mm Argon + 6mm tempered Low-E).

Can acoustic sliding doors have slim sightline frames?

Yes. JZZ1 engineers minimalist slim-frame acoustic sliding doors with interlock stiles as narrow as 20mm to 30mm, while accommodating heavy insulated glass up to 36mm thick and supporting sash weights up to 400 kg via heavy-duty stainless steel tandem rollers.

What information is needed for a factory-direct acoustic door RFQ?

To receive an itemized quote, send the door opening schedule, target acoustic rating (STC or Rw dB), glass build-up preference, wind load requirements (water tightness & Pa rating), and destination port.
