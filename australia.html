NCC & BCA Compliance Guide

Importing windows into Australia requires strict alignment with Australian building regulations. Here is how JZZ1 systems meet the technical expectations of Australian certifiers:

  • AS 4055 Wind Loads for Housing: Certified ratings up to 4000 Pa ULS cover housing wind classifications N1 to N5, and non-cyclonic high wind zones.
  • AS 1288 Glazing Code: Toughened or laminated safety glass installed in all critical human-impact zones (doors, side panels, low-sill windows <500mm).
  • Bushfire Attack Level (BAL): Systems compatible with BAL-12.5 up to BAL-40 fire ratings using toughened glass and stainless steel flyscreens.
  • Energy Efficiency & WERS: Double glazed argon units with warm-edge spacers achieving total window U-values down to 1.5–1.9 W/(m²·K).

Frequently Asked Questions for Australian Builders & Importers

Key compliance, engineering, and logistics answers regarding AS 2047 and AS/NZS 2208 factory-direct imports:

Why is AS 2047 mandatory for importing windows into Australia?

The National Construction Code (NCC) and Building Code of Australia (BCA) mandate that all external windows and glazed doors meet AS 2047 testing standards. Building certifiers require verified compliance certificates (such as our StandardsMark Licence SMK41678) before signing off on occupancy or building approvals.

What glass certification is required under Australian Standards?

All glass in windows and doors must comply with AS 1288 (Selection and installation of glass in buildings) and AS/NZS 2208 (Safety glazing materials). JZZ1 glass is certified under CSi Licence 7632 with permanent laser or ceramic safety stamps on every toughened and laminated panel.

What wind pressure levels are JZZ1 AS 2047 windows tested to?

Our MD75S tilt-and-turn system is certified to 4000 Pa Ultimate Limit State (ULS) wind pressure, 1600 Pa Serviceability Limit State (SLS), and 600 Pa water penetration resistance. The MD76A awning system is certified to 3000 Pa ULS and 200 Pa water penetration resistance.

How does JZZ1 pack windows for ocean shipment to Australian ports?

We utilize fumigated ISPM 15 compliant plywood crates with heavy steel strapping, corner edge protectors, waterproof plastic wrap, and moisture-absorbing desiccant packs. Crates are delivered direct to Port of Sydney, Port of Melbourne, Brisbane Port, or Fremantle.

Can you supply aluminum windows for Australia without using a local fabricator?

Yes. JZZ1 provides complete factory-glazed and fully assembled window and door units manufactured strictly to Australian Standards AS 2047 and AS/NZS 2208. Australian builders, developers, and trade importers source directly from our factory in Jinan, cutting intermediate local fabricator markups while receiving official compliance documentation (StandardsMark Licence SMK41678 and CSi Licence 7632) directly for building certifier sign-off.

What documents are provided for Australian customs clearance and certifier approval?

JZZ1 provides a complete Australian compliance documentation package for every export order: (1) Verified Intertek SAI Global StandardsMark Licence SMK41678 schedule; (2) CSi AS/NZS 2208 safety glazing test certificates with permanent panel stamp marks; (3) NATA accredited test reports (AS 4420 testing methods); (4) Form 15 compliance support; (5) ISPM 15 fumigation certificate for Australian Department of Agriculture biosecurity clearance; and (6) Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and Bill of Lading itemized by window schedule IDs.

What is the difference between 6063-T5 and 6061-T6 aluminum profiles for Australian building codes?

6063-T5 is the primary architectural extrusion alloy used for residential and commercial window/door frames, offering superior surface finish for Qualicoat powder coating and sharp corner detail. 6061-T6 provides higher structural tensile strength and is utilized for heavy structural mullions, large curtain wall transoms, or high-rise spans exposed to extreme wind pressures. JZZ1 specifies wall thickness from 1.4mm to 2.5mm+ to ensure compliance with NCC wind load ratings (N1 to N5, C1 to C2).

Can JZZ1 windows meet Australian Bushfire Attack Level (BAL) ratings?

Yes. JZZ1 systems can be engineered in compliance with AS 3959 for bushfire-prone zones including BAL-12.5, BAL-19, BAL-29, and up to BAL-40. Configurations utilize non-combustible aluminum frames, minimum 5mm or 6mm toughened safety glass or double glazed Low-E units, fire-retardant silicone seals, and stainless steel grade 316 protective insect/ember screens.
