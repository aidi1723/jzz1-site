Where energy efficiency comes from

Energy performance depends on the full configuration (frame, glass, seals, and hardware). Share your market and climate needs and we will recommend a configuration for confirmation.

  • Thermal break frames: reduce heat transfer through the aluminum frame.
  • Low-E IGU options: improve insulation and solar control.
  • Triple glazing (optional): for cold climates and higher performance targets.
  • Seals & hardware: multi-point locking and upgraded gaskets support air-tightness.

Verified NFRC Thermal Ratings (CPD: ESD-M-1), AS 2047 & International EPD (S-P-10194)

Energy code compliance in the United States (IECC / California Title 24), Australia (NCC Section J / NatHERS), and Europe (EN ISO 10077) requires documented laboratory simulation and physical thermal chamber testing:

  • US NFRC Product Certificate of Authorization (CPD: ESD-M-1): Issued via Keystone Certifications & Architectural Testing Inc. (Simulation Report SATI Q1366.01-116-45 R0, Physical Test TATI Q1367.01-116-46) for our ES70 Dual-Action Tilt & Turn series, achieving U-Factor 0.24–0.27 BTU/(hr·ft²·°F) (Uw 1.36–1.53 W/m²·K) and SHGC 0.42–0.47 (Valid to 2029-01-23).
  • Australia AS 2047 StandardsMark (SMK41678) & AS/NZS 2208 (CSi 7632): High air-tightness compression sealing tested up to ULS 4000 Pa and 600 Pa water resistance to eliminate uncontrolled HVAC air leakage.
  • The International EPD® System (S-P-10194) & FSC® (GFA-COC-007174): Third-party verified ISO 14025 and EN 15804 Life-Cycle Assessment (LCA) documentation for LEED, BREEAM, and Green Star commercial developments.
