Certified Thermal Break System Performance: US NFRC (CPD: ESD-M-1) & AU AS 2047 (SMK41678)

JZZ1 complete thermal break window and door systems are laboratory-tested as integrated assemblies—combining 6063-T5/T6 frames, PA66 GF25 thermal barriers, EPDM gaskets, Low-E Argon IGUs, and multi-point locking hardware:

  • US NFRC Fenestration Certification (CPD: ESD-M-1): ES70 Dual-Action Tilt & Turn thermal break system certified at U-Factor 0.24–0.27 BTU/(hr·ft²·°F) and SHGC 0.42–0.47 (Simulation Report SATI Q1366.01-116-45 R0, Physical Test TATI Q1367.01-116-46, valid to 2029-01-23).
  • Australia AS 2047-2014 StandardsMark (SMK41678) & AS/NZS 2208 (CSi 7632): MD75S Tilt & Turn system certified for ULS 4000 Pa wind pressure and 600 Pa water resistance; MD76A Awning & Fixed rated at ULS 3000 Pa.
  • ISO 9001:2015 (39726Q00006R001 & 10425Q00700R0S), AkzoNobel IATF 16949 (0561802) & EPD (S-P-10194): Complete factory quality control, automotive-grade coating consistency, and ISO 14025 environmental product declarations.
