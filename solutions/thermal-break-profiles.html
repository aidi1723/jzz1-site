Certified Thermal Break Extrusion Quality, AkzoNobel Coating & EPD Credentials

Window fabricators, curtain wall contractors, and architectural system houses rely on JZZ1 for precision roll-crimped thermal break profiles with full certification traceability:

  • ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management (39726Q00006R001) & International EPD® (S-P-10194): Extruded from primary 6063-T5, 6063-T6, and 6061-T6 billets to ASTM B221 / EN 755-9 tolerances with third-party verified ISO 14025 / EN 15804 LCA environmental declarations.
  • AkzoNobel IATF 16949 (0561802) & PPG Cationic E-Coat (CP525D): Dual-color (bicolor interior/exterior) or uniform powder coating using genuine AkzoNobel Interpon powders (LRQA 10559523) and PPG electrophoretic primer.
  • System-Level Fenestration Validation: Proven in our US NFRC (CPD: ESD-M-1, U-Factor 0.24–0.27) and Australian AS 2047 (SMK41678, ULS 4000 Pa) certified window series.
