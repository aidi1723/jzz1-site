Certified Safety Glazing (AS/NZS 2208 CSi 7632) & NFRC Thermal Performance (CPD: ESD-M-1)

Every Low-E Insulated Glazing Unit (IGU) assembled on JZZ1's automated dual-seal production line is backed by international safety and thermal testing:

  • AS/NZS 2208:1996 Safety Glazing Materials in Buildings (CSi Licence 7632): Certified by Certification Solutions International Pty Ltd (JAS-ANZ accredited) for Grade A toughened and laminated safety glass.
  • US NFRC Fenestration Certification (CPD: ESD-M-1): Validated by ATI simulation (SATI Q1366.01-116-45 R0) and physical test (TATI Q1367.01-116-46) achieving whole-window U-Factor 0.24–0.27 BTU/(hr·ft²·°F) and SHGC 0.42–0.47.
  • Automated Dual-Seal & Argon Gas Retention: Primary polyisobutylene (PIB) butyl vapor barrier paired with secondary structural silicone or polysulfide edge seal and 3A molecular sieve desiccant ensures > 90% Argon concentration stability.
