NFRC Thermal Baseline (CPD: ESD-M-1) & AS/NZS 2208 Safety Glazing (CSi 7632) for Triple IGUs

Triple-glazed insulated glass units are engineered for cold-climate residential villas, Passive House-inspired builds, and high-acoustic commercial facades:

  • Verified NFRC Thermal Architecture (CPD: ESD-M-1): Built upon our NFRC-certified thermal break platform (SATI Q1366.01-116-45 R0 / TATI Q1367.01-116-46), upgrading from double to dual-Low-E triple glazing lowers whole-window Uw down to 0.85–1.08 W/(m²·K) (U-Factor 0.15–0.19).
  • AS/NZS 2208:1996 Certified Safety Glass (CSi Licence 7632): All three panes—including the heat-absorbing center pane—are toughened or laminated to prevent thermal shock fracture and satisfy human-impact building codes.
  • Heavy-Load Sash Hardware (130–160 kg Window / 400 kg Door): Because 38mm–46mm triple IGUs add 50% more glass weight than double glazing, we engineer dedicated glazing rebates and heavy-duty multi-point hinges and rollers.
