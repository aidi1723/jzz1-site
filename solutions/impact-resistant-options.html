Verified ULS 4000 Pa Structural Wind Rating (AS 2047 SMK41678) & Safety Glazing (AS/NZS 2208 CSi 7632)

Coastal villas, tropical resorts, and high-rise towers in cyclone and hurricane corridors require verified frame deflection limits, deep glazing capture, and laminated impact glass:

  • AS 2047-2014 StandardsMark Licence (SMK41678): Certified by Intertek SAI Global with our MD75S series tested to 4000 Pa Ultimate Limit State (ULS) wind pressure and 600 Pa water penetration resistance, and MD76A rated to ULS 3000 Pa.
  • AS/NZS 2208:1996 Certified Laminated Safety Glass (CSi Licence 7632): Heavy-duty PVB and structural ionomer (SGP) laminated IGUs that maintain building envelope integrity if struck by windborne debris.
  • Coastal Anti-Corrosion Protection (AkzoNobel IATF 0561802 & PPG E-Coat CP525D): Impact zones are almost always coastal salt-spray zones; our frames combine PPG cationic epoxy primer with AkzoNobel Interpon / PVDF topcoats and 316 stainless steel hardware.
