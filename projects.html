Executive Export Project Capability Summary

From our 10,000+ m² manufacturing base in Jinan, Shandong, China, Jinan Jingzao New Materials Co., Ltd. (JZZ1) delivers complete drawing-to-container architectural aluminum packages for residential developers, commercial facade contractors, hospitality builders, and regional distributors:

  • Verified Third-Party Certifications: Intertek SAI Global AS 2047-2014 Licence SMK41678 (MD75S Sliding Window: ULS 4000 Pa, SLS 1500 Pa, Water 600 Pa; MD76A Awning Window: ULS 3000 Pa, SLS 2000 Pa, Water 200 Pa) and AS/NZS 2208:1996 Safety Glazing Licence CSi 7632.
  • Climate-Engineered System Range: Non-thermal heavy-duty typhoon sliding/awning systems for Southeast Asia (3.0–4.5 kPa), thermal break PA66 GF25 double/triple Low-E windows for the United States, Europe, and Southern Australia (Uw 0.80–1.50 W/(m²·K)), and unitized/stick curtain walls up to 5.0 kPa.
  • Container-Ready Export Packaging: Every unit is shop-assembled and trial-tested prior to packing, labeled by floor and elevation code (W-01, D-02) matching the architect's schedule, and loaded into fumigation-free plywood crates or steel A-frames for 20GP / 40HQ sea freight.
