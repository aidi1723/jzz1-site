🇺🇸 United States (CBP, ISF 10+2 & NFRC)

  • ISF (10+2) Filing: Transmitted to US CBP at least 24 hours before vessel departure from Qingdao. JZZ1 supplies complete factory MID and container stuffing manifests promptly.

  • Energy & Building Codes: Supported by verified NFRC CPD ESD-M-1 authorization (U-Factor 0.24–0.27).

Frequently Asked Questions

Importing, HS Codes, Preferential Duties & Clearance FAQ

What is the standard HS code for importing architectural aluminum windows and doors?

The international 6-digit Harmonized System classification for finished architectural aluminum doors, windows, and their frames is HS 7610.10. Hollow extruded framing profiles fall under HS 7604.21, curtain wall structural assemblies fall under HS 7610.90, and multiple-walled insulating glass units fall under HS 7008.00.

Who handles import customs clearance under FOB vs CIF vs DDP terms?

Under FOB Qingdao and CIF terms, JZZ1 handles China export declaration, origin terminal handling charges (THC), and ISPM 15 crating, while the buyer handles destination port import customs clearance and local duty/tax payment. Under DDP terms, JZZ1 and our partner customs broker coordinate end-to-end ocean freight, import clearance, duties, and jobsite delivery.

Does JZZ1 provide Certificates of Origin (ChAFTA / Form E) to reduce import tariffs?

Yes. JZZ1 issues official Preferential Certificates of Origin via China Customs and CCPIT, including ChAFTA Certificates of Origin for Australia (reducing eligible window import duty from 5% MFN to 0%), China-ASEAN FTA Form E for Philippines, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, and Vietnam, and RCEP or non-preferential Certificates of Origin for global destinations.

Does JZZ1 comply with ISPM 15 wood packaging and Australian BMSB quarantine rules?

Yes. JZZ1 utilizes manufactured engineered plywood crates (quarantine-exempt processed wood) paired with ISPM 15 heat-treated (HT) IPPC-stamped solid timber skids or heavy-duty steel transport racks. For seasonal Australian BMSB shipments (September 1 to April 30), we configure 100% steel-rack or engineered-plywood packaging to prevent onshore fumigation delays.

What documentation does JZZ1 provide for US and Australian import clearance?

For US shipments, JZZ1 supplies manufacturer details and container stuffing records for ISF (10+2) filing at least 24 hours prior to Qingdao loading, NFRC CPD ESD-M-1 authorization certificates, and clear finished-merchandise invoices under HS 7610.10.0000. For Australia, JZZ1 provides AS 2047 StandardsMark Licence SMK41678 documentation, AS/NZS 2208 safety glazing certificates (CSi Licence 7632), ChAFTA CO, and Form 15 schedules.

How much ocean freight volume and weight fit into a 20GP vs 40HQ container?

Assembled glazed windows typically occupy ~0.12 CBM and weigh ~32–42 kg gross per square meter, allowing approximately 210–230 m² inside a 20GP container (~28 CBM / 21,500 kg payload cap) or 500–560 m² inside a 40HQ container (~68 CBM / 26,500 kg payload cap). For glass-only IGU shipments, JZZ1 Scheme 6 horizontal crating packs up to 615.55 m² (600 panes, 16.68 metric tons) into a single 20GP container.
