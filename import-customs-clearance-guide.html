Country-Specific Clearance & Quarantine Compliance

🇺🇸 United States (CBP Requirements)

  • ISF 10+2 Importer Security Filing: Must be filed 24 hours prior to container vessel departure from Qingdao Port. JZZ1 provides complete manufacturer identity and stuffing confirmation in advance.
  • NFRC Energy Compliance: JZZ1 holds verified CPD Number ESD-M-1 covering ES70 tilt & turn and casement windows (U-Factor 0.24–0.27, SHGC 0.42–0.47) for US building energy code approvals.
  • HS Tariff: Aluminum fenestration is entered under HS 7610.10.0000.

🇦🇺 Australia & New Zealand (ABF & DAFF)

  • Mandatory AS 2047 StandardsMark: Verified Licence SMK41678 audited by Intertek SAI Global, covering MD75S and MD76A systems with certified Form 15 compliance.
  • CSi Licence 7632 Safety Glazing: Insulated and laminated glass marked in full compliance with AS/NZS 2208:1996.
  • BMSB Stink Bug Protocol (Sep 1 to Apr 30): We utilize 100% processed engineered plywood crates with IPPC ISPM 15 standards, exempt from mandatory onshore fumigation delays.

🇸🇬 Singapore (BCA & TradeNet)

  • BCA Green Mark Acoustic Isolation: Tested STC 35–44 dB acoustic damping and SS 341 window testing alignment for condominium and hotel projects.
  • Tariffs & Taxes: 0% import duty (Singapore is a free port for aluminum doors & windows); 9% GST applies upon inward permit issuance via TradeNet.

🇪🇺 European Union & UK (CE / EPD)

  • Environmental Product Declaration (EPD): Registered under The International EPD System (Registration: S-P-10194) for LEED and BREEAM sustainability credits.
  • Automotive-Grade Coating: AkzoNobel IATF 16949 (Certificate: 0561802) and PPG Powercron® CP525D e-coat primer.
  • Importer Requirements: Importer must possess an active EORI number for EU customs declaration.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions on Importing & Customs Clearance

What is the standard HS code for importing architectural aluminum windows and doors?

The standard 6-digit Harmonized System classification for architectural aluminum doors, windows and their frames is HS 7610.10. Associated hollow extruded framing profiles fall under HS 7604.21, and curtain wall structural components fall under HS 7610.90.

Who handles import customs clearance under FOB vs CIF terms?

Under both FOB Qingdao and CIF terms, the buyer is responsible for destination port import customs clearance, duty payment, and local port taxes. However, JZZ1 provides complete certified commercial documentation (dual-language Commercial Invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, and bill of lading data) to ensure smooth clearance. Under DDP terms, JZZ1 and our designated partner broker handle end-to-end import clearance and duty payment.

Does JZZ1 comply with ISPM 15 wood packaging and fumigation standards?

Yes. JZZ1 exclusively utilizes manufactured non-fumigation engineered plywood crates and heavy-duty steel transport racks. These processed materials are fully exempt from raw wood quarantine restrictions and comply with ISPM 15 worldwide, avoiding costly destination fumigation delays even during Australia's BMSB seasonal quarantine window.

What documentation does JZZ1 provide for US and Australian import clearance?

For US shipments, JZZ1 supplies manufacturer container stuffing data for ISF (10+2) filing 24h prior to vessel loading, along with NFRC CPD ESD-M-1 test reports. For Australian shipments, JZZ1 provides StandardsMark Licence SMK41678 documentation, AS/NZS 2208 safety glazing certificates (CSi 7632), and certified Form 15 compliance schedules.
