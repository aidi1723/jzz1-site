Singapore Green Mark and Acoustic Window Guide

This technical guide provides engineering specifications, BCA Green Mark & SS 341 compliance criteria, STC 35–44 dB acoustic glazing configurations, and TradeNet import clearance guidance for Singapore condominium, hotel, and commercial projects.

Direct Engineering Answer

For Singapore high-rise condominiums and hospitality developments, JZZ1 supplies 6063-T5 thermal break aluminum systems (W70, W80, CY156) achieving STC 35–44 dB acoustic attenuation, SHGC 0.22–0.35 solar-control Low-E glazing for BCA Green Mark ETTV/RETV compliance, and 600 Pa monsoon water tightness. Shipments clear PSA Singapore in 8–11 days from Qingdao Port at 0% import customs duty under HS 7610.10.

BCA Green Mark & SS 341 Inspection Checkpoints

  • ETTV & RETV Solar Control: Double-silver Low-E coatings reduce solar heat gain without sacrificing visible light transmittance (VLT ≥ 50%), lowering HVAC cooling loads.
  • Tropical Monsoon Water Tightness: Stepped drainage tracks and vulcanized EPDM triple-gasket sealing tested up to 600 Pa water penetration prevent wind-driven rain ingress.
  • High-Rise Wind Deflection: 6063-T5/T6 reinforced mullions engineered for 3000–4500 Pa ULS design wind pressure with minimal sash deflection.
  • Green Material Traceability: Backed by The International EPD System (S-P-10194), ISO 9001:2015, and AkzoNobel IATF 16949 powder coating durability.

Frequently Asked Questions

What acoustic rating (STC) do Singapore condos and expressway-facing apartments typically require?

High-density Singapore condominiums and hotels near expressways or MRT lines typically specify STC 35 to 44 dB. JZZ1 achieves this using multi-chamber 6063-T5 thermal break frames (W70/W80/CY156) paired with acoustic laminated IGUs (such as 6mm + 1.14mm/1.52mm PVB + 6mm + 12A + 6mm) and triple EPDM compression gaskets.

How do JZZ1 windows support Singapore BCA Green Mark ETTV/RETV targets?

BCA Green Mark assessments focus on Envelope Thermal Transfer Value (ETTV/RETV) and solar heat gain. JZZ1 supplies Low-E double-silver glazed units with SHGC tuned between 0.22 and 0.35 and whole-window U-values down to 1.36–1.53 W/(m²·K), supported by International EPD S-P-10194 sustainability documentation.

What are the import duties and transit times when shipping windows from Qingdao to Singapore?

Singapore is a free port for architectural aluminum windows and doors under HS 7610.10, meaning 0% import customs duty (only 9% GST applies upon TradeNet inward permit). Direct FCL ocean transit from Qingdao Port to PSA Singapore takes approximately 8 to 11 days.

What structural and safety standards apply to Singapore high-rise windows (SS 341)?

High-rise residential and hospitality projects require verified wind-load deflection resistance, water tightness against tropical monsoon rain, and safety glazing. JZZ1 systems are tested up to 4000–4500 Pa ULS wind load, 600 Pa water penetration, and include laminated or toughened safety glass.
