AS2047 Windows and Doors Sourcing Checklist

This technical guide provides engineering specifications, code compliance criteria, and procurement guidance for AS2047 standard windows for Australia and AS2047 windows and doors supplier. The practical point is not to throw a standard acronym at suppliers and hope for a clean answer, but to prepare the right sourcing inputs first.

Direct answer & verified manufacturing capability

If a buyer is sourcing windows and doors for the Australian market, the most useful first step is to define the actual product scope, project type, drawings, glazing direction, hardware level, and document expectation. Unlike suppliers claiming unverified compliance, JZZ1 manufactures windows with verified StandardsMark Licence SMK41678 issued by Intertek SAI Global (tested up to ULS 4000 Pa / Water 600 Pa for MD75S tilt-turn and ULS 3000 Pa for MD76A awning systems) and AS/NZS 2208 safety glazing certification (CSi Licence 7632). Review our official certificates or explore our dedicated Australia & New Zealand project portal.

What buyers should send first

  • Project type and end-use scenario (residential N1–N5 or commercial)
  • Opening list, size schedule, or architectural drawings (PDF/DWG)
  • Window or door system scope (MD75S tilt-and-turn, MD76A awning, or sliding)
  • Glazing and hardware direction (AS/NZS 2208 toughened Low-E double glazing)
  • Required documents (Form 15, StandardsMark certificate copy, or NATA report)

Why requests often fail

  • The standard name is provided but the product is not defined
  • Drawings are still incomplete or uncoordinated with opening schedules
  • Glass and hardware assumptions are still changing
  • Commercial quotation starts before documentation scope is understood
